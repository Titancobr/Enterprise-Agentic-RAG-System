Article 6 — Access to Genetic Resources
Access to genetic resources shall be subject to prior informed consent (PIC) of the Party providing such resources... unless otherwise determined by that Party.

Article 7 — Access to Traditional Knowledge Associated with Genetic Resources
Access to traditional knowledge associated with genetic resources shall be subject to prior informed consent... of the indigenous and local communities concerned.

Article 15 — Compliance with Domestic Legislation on ABS
Each Party shall take appropriate measures... to ensure that genetic resources utilized within its jurisdiction have been accessed with PIC and MAT (mutually agreed terms).

Article 16 — Compliance with Traditional Knowledge ABS
Parties shall take measures to ensure that traditional knowledge associated with genetic resources utilized within their jurisdiction has been accessed with PIC and MAT.

Article 17 — Model Contractual Clauses
Parties shall encourage the development of model contractual clauses for MAT...

[Explanation: The Nagoya Protocol implements the CBD's ABS provisions. India's BD Act 2023/2024 Rules implement Nagoya domestically. For international patent filings, disclosure of origin and PIC/MAT compliance is increasingly required by major patent offices.]
