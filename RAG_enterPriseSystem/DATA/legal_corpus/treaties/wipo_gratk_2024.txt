Article 3 — Disclosure Requirement for Genetic Resources
Where a patent application claims an invention based on genetic resources, the applicant shall disclose:
(a) the country of origin of the genetic resources; or
(b) where the country of origin is not known, the source of the genetic resources.

Article 4 — Disclosure Requirement for Traditional Knowledge
Where a patent application claims an invention based on traditional knowledge associated with genetic resources, the applicant shall disclose:
(a) the Indigenous Peoples or local community who provided the traditional knowledge; or
(b) where the providers are not known, the source of the traditional knowledge.

Article 5 — Implementation of the Disclosure Requirement
Contracting Parties shall provide measures to ensure compliance with the disclosure requirement... Failure to disclose shall not result in invalidation/unenforceability unless fraudulent intent.

Article 6 — Information Systems
Contracting Parties may establish databases of genetic resources and associated traditional knowledge... Access subject to authorization.

[Explanation: The GRATK Treaty (adopted May 2024) creates the first international legal framework linking patent disclosure to genetic resources and traditional knowledge. India, as a megadiverse country and TK holder, is a key proponent. Once ratified, Indian patent law will need to align (Form 18A is a precursor).]
