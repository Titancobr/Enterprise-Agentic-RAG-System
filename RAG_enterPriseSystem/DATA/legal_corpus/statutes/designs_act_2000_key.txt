Designs Act, 2000 — Packaging, Containers, and Bottles for Ayurvedic Products

1. Protection of Packaging Designs:
Under the Designs Act 2000, features of shape, configuration, pattern, ornament, or composition of lines or colours applied to any article (including unique bottles, containers, dispensers, and packaging boxes for Ayurvedic formulations) can be registered.

2. Novelty and Originality Requirement (Section 4):
A design cannot be registered if it is not new or original, or has been disclosed to the public anywhere in India or abroad prior to filing, or is not significantly distinguishable from known designs or combinations of known designs.

3. Term of Protection (Section 11):
Registration of a design confers copyright in the design for an initial period of 10 years from the date of registration, extendable for a second period of 5 years upon application and payment of prescribed fees (total 15 years).

4. Distinction from Trademarks and Patents:
Design registration protects the aesthetic external visual appearance and industrial ornamentation of the container or packaging. It does not protect the internal Ayurvedic formulation, therapeutic mechanism, or brand name/logo (which fall under patents and trademarks respectively). Combining design registration with trademark protection provides comprehensive brand defense.
