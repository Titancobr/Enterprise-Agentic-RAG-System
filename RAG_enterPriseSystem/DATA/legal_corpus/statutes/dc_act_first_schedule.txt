First Schedule — List of authoritative books of Ayurvedic, Siddha and Unani systems

A. Ayurveda:
1. Charaka Samhita
2. Sushruta Samhita
3. Ashtanga Hridaya
4. Ashtanga Sangraha
5. Bhavaprakasha
6. Madhava Nidana
7. Sharangadhara Samhita
8. Bhaisajya Ratnavali
9. Chakradatta
10. Yogaratnakara
... (and others notified by Central Government)

[Explanation: Formulations whose composition and method of preparation are EXACTLY as described in these texts are "Classical Ayurvedic Medicines." They are listed in the First Schedule and enjoy:
- Exemption from clinical trial requirements for licensing (Rule 158B)
- No patent eligibility for the formulation itself (Section 3(p) bar)
- GI protection possible for region-specific classical formulations
- ABS exemption for vaids/hakims practicing for livelihood (BD Act Section 55)]

Proprietary Ayurvedic Medicines (non-classical):
- Not in First Schedule
- Require licensing under AYUSH/State Drug Controller
- May be patentable if novel composition/process (subject to Section 3(p))
- Require safety data (Rule 158B) — may need non-clinical / clinical evidence
