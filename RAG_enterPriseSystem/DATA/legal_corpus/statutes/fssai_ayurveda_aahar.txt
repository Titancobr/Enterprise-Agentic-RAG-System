Definitions:
- "Ayurveda Aahar" means food prepared in accordance with the recipes/ingredients/methods described in authoritative Ayurvedic texts listed in Schedule I of these regulations.
- "Ayurveda Aahar" does not include drugs as defined under Drugs & Cosmetics Act.

Categories:
1. Ayurveda Aahar — Foods based on classical recipes, no therapeutic claims
2. Proprietary Ayurveda Aahar — New formulations, require safety assessment
3. Food supplements / Nutraceuticals — Separate FSSAI regulations apply

Key Requirements:
- No disease treatment, cure, or prevention claims (Section 24 FSS Act)
- Labeling must comply with FSS (Packaging & Labelling) Regulations
- Ingredients must be from permitted list (Schedule II)
- Manufacturing license from State Food Authority
- GMP compliance per Schedule 4

[Explanation: The Ayurveda Aahar route is for food/nutraceutical positioning. Lower regulatory burden than drug pathway, but NO patent protection for formulation, and NO therapeutic claims permitted. Suitable for wellness products, not medicines.]
