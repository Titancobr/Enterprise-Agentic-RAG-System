Section 3(p) — What are not inventions

An invention which, in effect, is traditional knowledge or which is an aggregation or duplication of known properties of traditionally known component or components.

[Explanation: This section creates a statutory bar on patenting traditional knowledge. For Ayurveda, this means classical formulations documented in authoritative texts (First Schedule of Drugs & Cosmetics Act) cannot be patented as products. Process patents may still be available if the manufacturing process is novel and non-obvious.]
