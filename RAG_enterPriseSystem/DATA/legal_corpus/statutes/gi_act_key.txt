Section 2(e) — "Geographical Indication" means an indication which identifies goods as originating in the territory of a country, or a region or locality in that territory, where a given quality, reputation or other characteristic of such goods is essentially attributable to its geographical origin.

Section 11 — Prohibition of registration of certain geographical indications
Cannot register GI if: generic, contrary to public order, not protected in country of origin, etc.

Section 13 — Registration confers rights
Registered proprietor and authorized users have exclusive right to use GI. Relief for infringement under Section 21.

[Explanation for Ayurveda: Region-specific classical formulations (e.g., "Kashmir Saffron", "Kerala Ayurvedic Oil", "Madhya Pradesh Ashwagandha") can be protected as GIs. Applications are filed with the Geographical Indications Registry located in Chennai. The process involves submitting evidence linking the product's quality, reputation, or characteristics to its geographic region, examination by the registry, publication in the GI Journal, and a 4-month opposition window. Registration is valid for 10 years (renewable). Registration confers exclusive rights to the registered proprietor and authorized users under Section 13 of the GI Act, preventing unauthorized commercial exploitation.]
