Drugs and Cosmetics Rules, 1945 — Rule 158B: Licensing of Patent or Proprietary Ayurvedic Medicines

1. Classification of Proprietary Ayurvedic Medicine:
A formulation that contains Ayurvedic ingredients mentioned in authoritative books specified in the First Schedule but is prepared in a modified composition, new combination, or modern dosage form (such as tablet, capsule, syrup instead of classical churna or kwatha) is classified as a Patent or Proprietary Ayurvedic Medicine.

2. Evidence Requirements for Licensing under Rule 158B:
Rule 158B specifies the evidentiary requirements for obtaining a manufacturing license from the State AYUSH Licensing Authority:
- Classical Ingredients with Published Literature: For traditional ingredients used in combinations, published safety and efficacy literature or documented references in authoritative books are required.
- New Combinations / Modified Forms: When known ingredients are combined in new ratios or modern dosage forms, proof of safety (acute and sub-acute toxicity studies) is mandatory.
- New Therapeutic Indications: If new therapeutic claims not documented in classical texts are made, proof of clinical safety and efficacy through clinical trials is required.
- Classical Formulations: Formulations prepared exactly according to classical texts (First Schedule) are exempt from clinical trial requirements.
- Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP): Compliance with Schedule T (GMP for ASU drugs) is mandatory for all licensed units.
