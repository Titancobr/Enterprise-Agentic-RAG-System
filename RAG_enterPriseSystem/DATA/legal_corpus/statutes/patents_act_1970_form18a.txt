Form 18A — Statement regarding source of biological material / traditional knowledge

Rule 13(8) requires every patent application involving biological material or traditional knowledge to disclose:
(a) the geographical origin of the biological material used in the invention;
(b) the traditional knowledge, if any, associated with the invention;
(c) whether prior informed consent and fair and equitable benefit sharing arrangements are in place.

[Explanation: This form operationalizes India's ABS obligations under the Nagoya Protocol. Failure to disclose or false disclosure is a ground for opposition (Section 25) and revocation (Section 64).]
